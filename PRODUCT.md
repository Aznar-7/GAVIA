# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, deployed on Vercel (chosen by the founders, 2026-10-10).

## Users

Primary: owners of small and medium businesses (pymes) and entrepreneurs in Córdoba and the rest of Argentina who need a software system built (management systems, web/mobile apps, integrations, automations) and are evaluating whom to hire. They usually run their operation on Excel, WhatsApp and paper today, are wary of being sold vapor, and compare a small studio against freelancers (Workana) and established software factories.

Secondary: truckers and cargo shippers arriving for Flety; startup programs and accelerators (e.g. Claude for Startups) checking that the team is real.

## Product Purpose

Gavia is a software studio from Córdoba, Argentina. It designs, builds, deploys and maintains custom software end to end (requirements elicitation, functional and non-functional requirements, use cases, architecture, code, deploy, maintenance) and builds its own products. Success for the website: a pyme owner reads it, trusts the team, and writes to them.

## Positioning

A small engineering team where the person who listens to you is the person who writes your code, with the formal engineering process of a large firm (documented requirements, architecture, staged deliveries, contracts) at pyme scale, plus their own products, which proves they find and solve real problems.

## Operating Context

Clients typically contact by WhatsApp or email. Typical engagement: discovery meeting → requirements document → proposal with closed price per stage → deliveries every two weeks → go-live with data migration and training → monthly maintenance plan. Pricing is quoted per project after discovery (no public prices).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services at launch: custom software development, and own products. IoT/hardware is NOT offered yet (do not show hardware projects or claims). Consulting not offered as a standalone line yet.
- Own product: **Flety** (working name, kept): a backhaul freight app so trucks don't return empty (any A → B route across Argentina; grain Córdoba → port is only an example). Uber/PedidosYa-style: loads and routes matched between truckers and shippers. In development; no users yet.
- Team: Vicente Aznar (2 years full-stack developer), Agustín García, possibly Benjamín Viberti (third founder, undecided). Engineering students in Córdoba.
- Not yet incorporated; corporate email and domain pending (gaviasoftware.com looked available).
- First meeting (discovery call) is free and without commitment (decided 2026-10-10).
- Undecided: final logo, guarantee terms.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Gavia** (provisional; from García · Aznar · Viberti + "IA"; the gavia is the topsail of a ship). Tagline candidate: "Software con rumbo."
- Voice: Rioplatense Spanish with voseo. Close, modern, but serious: the professionalism of Globant or Mercado Libre with the closeness of a brother or friend. Plain words, no buzzwords ("soluciones innovadoras", "potenciamos", "transformación digital"), no emojis.
- Explicitly rejected by the founders: AI-slop aesthetics (everything rounded, emojis, everything centered, purple gradients, generic icon cards).
- Market: Argentina first, global in the distant future.

## Evidence on Hand

Real projects to show: UTN Hub (academic management system with an automated course-enrollment bot), a record player built with a Raspberry Pi, a car built with Arduino. No clients, testimonials, metrics or case studies yet. Never fabricate testimonials, client logos, counters or statistics.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with process and commitments, not adjectives: every stage ends in something the client can read, test or sign.
2. Say only what the team will honor; every promise on the site is a contract.
3. Closeness is structural: the people who answer are the people who build.
4. Focus over breadth: few things, done well.
